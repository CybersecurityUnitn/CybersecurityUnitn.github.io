## Capture the Flag activity at UniTN

We participate in the [CyberChallenge.IT programme](https://cyberchallenge.it/) 2027; registrations open in December. We run a training programme for a selected number of students.

Lectures take place in DISI rooms. Students admitted to the programme receive an email with further details; additional information is available on the [UniTN venue page](https://cyberchallenge.it/venues/unitn).

In 2023 and 2026, we ranked 2nd in the [national competition final](https://cyberchallenge.it/halloffame/2026).

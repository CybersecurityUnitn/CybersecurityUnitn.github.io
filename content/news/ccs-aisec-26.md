title: "UniTN at CCS 2026 and AISec 2026"
date: 2026-09-10
---

Matteo Golinelli and Carlo Ramponi will travel to The Hague to present two accepted papers at ACM CCS 2026 and its colocated AISec workshop. Carlo will present [Mitigating Code Injection Attacks on Modern GPUs](/publication/gpu-code-injection/), while Matteo will present [AgentLSD: Evaluating AI Security Agents Under Adversarial Task Contamination](/publication/agent-lsd/).

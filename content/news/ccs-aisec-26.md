Matteo Golinelli and Carlo Ramponi will travel to The Hague to present two accepted papers at ACM CCS 2026 and its colocated AISec workshop. Carlo will present [N00B: Bounds Checking for the Masses](/publication/n00b/), while Matteo will present [AgentLSD: Evaluating AI Security Agents Under Adversarial Task Contamination](/publication/agent-lsd/).

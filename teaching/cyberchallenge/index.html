Capture the Flag activity at UniTN

We participate in the CyberChallenge.IT programme 2027; registrations open in December. We run a training programme for a selected number of students.

Lectures take place in DISI rooms. Students admitted to the programme receive an email with further details; additional information is available on the UniTN venue page.

In 2023 and 2026, we ranked 2nd in the national competition final.
